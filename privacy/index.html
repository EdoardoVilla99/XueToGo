Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

XueToGo is a Mandarin flashcard app. We are committed to your privacy. This policy explains, plainly, what data the app does and does not collect.

What we collect

Nothing. XueToGo does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. The app has no servers, no user accounts, and no analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.

Purchases

In-app purchases are handled entirely by Apple via StoreKit. We never see or store your payment information. Apple's privacy policy governs those transactions: apple.com/legal/privacy.

Local storage

Your flashcard progress and app preferences are saved locally on your device using standard iOS APIs. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us.

Third-party SDKs

The app does not include any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

Children

XueToGo does not collect information from anyone, including children. Because no data is collected, the app is safe for users of all ages.

Changes to this policy

If we ever change these practices we will update this page and revise the "Last updated" date above.

Contact

Questions? Email us at privacy@xuetogo.app.
