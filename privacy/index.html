Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 August 2026

XueToGo does not collect, transmit or store any personal data. The app has no servers, no user accounts and no analytics. It works entirely offline.

What is stored on your device

The following is saved locally on your iPhone and is never sent anywhere:

  • Your learning progress (which words you have marked as learned or mastered)
  • Favourites, daily streak and calendar of study days
  • Preferences: language, script, daily goal, reminder times, audio settings

What is not collected

  • No name, email address or account of any kind
  • No location data
  • No contacts, photos, microphone or camera access
  • No advertising identifiers (IDFA)
  • No tracking across other apps or websites
  • No third-party analytics or advertising SDKs

Notifications

If you enable practice reminders, they are scheduled locally by iOS on your device. No notification is sent from a server, and no notification data leaves your phone.

Purchases

XueToGo Plus subscriptions are processed entirely by Apple through StoreKit. Apple handles payment; XueToGo only receives an indication of whether your subscription is currently active. Payment details are never visible to, or stored by, this app. Apple's handling of that transaction is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children

XueToGo does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children under 13. Because no data is collected at all, there is nothing to collect from any age group.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app from your device permanently removes all stored progress and preferences. No copy of your data exists anywhere else, so there is nothing further to request or erase.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new "last updated" date.
